FAQ

My 3D printer or slicer gives an error when I load the ZIP

Extract the ZIP first and load plate_XX.obj or plate_XX.stl. → Steps

A kanji reading is wrong

Tap the braille cell in the preview and correct the reading in hiragana. → Correcting a reading

“Many characters cannot be converted” appears

The braille language may not match your text. → About the warning

I can’t find the saved file

If you chose “Save to Files”, it is where you saved it in the Files app.
