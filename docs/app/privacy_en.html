Privacy Policy

Tenji P-Fab (“the App”) does not collect any personal information and does not communicate with external servers.

Effective date: 2026-10-07

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, e-mail address, device identifiers or location, nor any usage data. No account is required.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

  • Input history: the text and settings from when you saved (the number of entries is adjustable). You can clear the history from the history screen; deleting the App removes everything.
  • Output settings: characters per line, plate thickness, etc.
  • Exported ZIP files: created when you tap Save. You choose where they are saved or shared (Files, AirDrop, etc.); how the destination handles them is governed by that service’s own terms and settings.

3. Communication

The App has no function that sends data over the Internet. Japanese text analysis and braille data generation are performed entirely on your device.

4. Disclosure to third parties

Because we collect no information, we provide none to third parties. The App contains no advertising SDKs or analytics tools.

5. Children’s privacy

Because the App collects no personal information, it can be used safely at any age.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
